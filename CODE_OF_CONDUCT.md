# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the `lsusers` community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accountability, and focus on the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized conduct, publishing private information, and other inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `matheusabreu.dev@gmail.com`.
